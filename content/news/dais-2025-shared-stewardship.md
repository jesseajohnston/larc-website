---
title: "DAIS to focus on Shared Stewardship in 2025"
date: 2025-01-08
---

The DAIS series will examine scholarship and/or practice of shared responsibility for and collaboration around cultural collections. The initiative explores partnership models, collections stewardship across community archives, Indigenous knowledge systems, and data reuse contexts.

Key questions include: Who shares responsibilities? Which communities participate? How are resources distributed? What partnership forms prove authentic and effective?

Planned speakers:

- Thuy Vo Dang (UCLA)
- Trevor Owens (American Institute of Physics)
- Estevan Rael-Galvez (Native Bound Unbound / SAR)
