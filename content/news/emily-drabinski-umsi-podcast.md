title: "Emily Drabinski Featured on UMSI Podcast!"
date: 2024-08-15
---

Emily Drabinski, former American Library Association President, delivered a talk co-sponsored by DAIS at the Rackham Amphitheatre. She subsequently appeared on the School of Information's new podcast, discussing critical librarianship and action within the field.

Check out the episode [here](https://www.si.umich.edu/about-umsi/podcasts/information-changes-everything-podcast/402-critical-librarianship-action-ala).
