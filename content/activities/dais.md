DAIS is an annual speaker series coordinated by the LARC Research Group. It features speakers whose work advances understanding in topics related to archival studies, librarianship, digital curation, and data, as it is understood and critiqued within the context of information environments and social concerns.

The series brings together scholars and practitioners working at the intersection of information science, archival studies, and social concerns. Past speakers have addressed topics including:

- Digital preservation and data management
- Community archives and marginalized representations
- Search and discovery in digital collections
- Information justice and equity
- Archival access and difficult histories
- Indigenous knowledge systems and documentation

The series is designed to foster dialogue between academic researchers, cultural heritage professionals, and community-based archivists on how data, archives, and information systems shape and reflect society.
