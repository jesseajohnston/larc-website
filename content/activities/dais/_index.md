LARC sponsors and coordinates an annual series of speakers and symposia called DAIS, or "Data, Archives, and Information in Society." DAIS features speakers whose work advances understanding in topics related to archival studies, librarianship, digital curation, and data, as it is understood and critiqued within the context of information environments and social concerns.

Upcoming speakers, dates, and talk information will be updated as information becomes available.
