LARC sponsors and coordinates a series of speakers and symposia called DAIS, or Data, Archives, and Information in Society. We invite speakers to the DAIS whose work advances understanding in topics related to archives, curation, data, and libraries, as it is understood and critiqued within the context of information environments and social concerns.

Upcoming speakers, dates, and talk information will be updated as information becomes available.
