Gracen Brilmyer (they/them) is a Disabled researcher working at the intersection of feminist disability studies and archival studies. Their work investigates the erasure of disabled people in archives primarily within the history of natural history museums and colonial histories as well as how disabled people experience themselves in archival material. They are currently assistant professor at McGill University and director of the Disability Archives Lab. Find a bibliography for Dr. Brilmyer's seminar talk at [disabilityarchiveslab.com/crippingabsence](http://disabilityarchiveslab.com/crippingabsence).
