## Research seminar

The LARC research seminar convenes each Fall and Winter semester, providing a venue where members present and refine their scholarly work. We generally focus on the work of currently enrolled doctoral students whose research centers on aspects of archival science, data science, digital curation, and social memory in varied knowledge environments. The seminar also features presentations on faculty research, recent publications, and specialized topics within library and information science, archives, and digital curation.

## Symposia & speakers

LARC organizes an annual speaker series and symposia program titled [Data, Archives, and Information in Society (DAIS)]({{< relref "activities/dais" >}}). The initiative invites speakers whose work advances understanding in topics related to archives, curation, data, and libraries, as it is understood and critiqued within the context of information environments and social concerns.
