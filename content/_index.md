---
title: "Libraries, Archives, and Curation"
---

The Libraries, Archives, and digital Curation research group, also known as LARC, explores core areas of information science related to archival studies, digital curation, recordkeeping, and library science. As such, the group convenes scholarly and research interests within the School of Information, and across campus, around these topics. The group's members engage in the exploration of a wide range of "knowledge environments" in society, such as cultural heritage and collections infrastructure (libraries, archives, and museums), government, health, and education, among other topics. We aim to focus conversations around complex socio-technical information questions related to digital curation, access, use, and discovery from a diverse array of research perspectives.

Our members work in a variety of research areas:

- Epistemic infrastructures and Knowledge environments
- Archival science and librarianship
- Community-based archives and recordkeeping
- Digital curation
- Digital libraries and collections
- Digital preservation
- Data literacy, curation, sharing, and reuse
- Information behavior
- Information equity/Information resilience
- Information literacy
- Information accessibility
- Museum informatics
- Public libraries
- Recordkeeping practices
- Human rights and social justice
- Reparative practices and approaches in cultural institutions
